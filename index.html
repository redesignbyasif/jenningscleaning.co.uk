About Jennings Cleaning

A trusted quality seal, since 2005

Founded by Scott Jennings in 2005, Jennings Cleaning had modest beginnings — a car, a ladder, a bucket and a squeegee. Two decades on, we operate from fully equipped vans fitted with class-leading Gardiner water-fed pole systems and a ready supply of in-house filtered, heated pure water.

Window cleaning sits at the very heart of what we do, but our care extends across the whole exterior of your property — and its interior too. Every job is finished with the same straightforward desire to please that built our reputation across Shepway.

The pure water difference

Why pure water changes everything

We carry our own onboard supply of water, purified by particle filtration and reverse osmosis, then heated for a deeper clean. It dries spotless — no chemicals, no streaks, no residue.

Heated pure water

Filtered, heated and 100% pure, our water lifts ground-in grime that cold tap water simply moves around.

Ground-based reach

Water-fed poles reach windows above conservatories and high-level glazing in multi-storey complexes — without ladders.

A spotless finish

Pure water dries without streaks, spots or residue — the glass stays cleaner for longer, naturally.

Safety first

Working from the ground means less ladder work and less risk — and your property is treated with the utmost respect.
